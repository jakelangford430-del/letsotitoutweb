Kenslee Oxley

Founder & Implementation Specialist

[Kenslee's Photo]

Kenslee's background is fundamentally different from typical consultants. She started her career running property management operations — managing portfolios, leading teams, handling clients, and learning the real complexities of a busy property management business from the ground up.

That operational experience shaped everything she does. She understands the challenges directors face: growing faster than your processes can handle, stretched leadership teams, systems that aren't delivering value, offshore staff integration issues, and the constant pressure to maintain service quality while controlling costs.

She then spent years working closely with property management software providers, learning enterprise systems from the inside and helping real agencies implement them properly. She discovered a critical gap: most operational problems aren't solved by software or consulting advice alone.

Real improvement requires experienced operational leadership that stays involved — diagnosing the actual issues, designing sustainable solutions, training your team to execute them, and holding everyone accountable until real change sticks.

The OCD PM was built on that insight: combining genuine operational experience with real implementation commitment. We don't hand you a report and leave. We step in, we fix it, and we make sure your team can sustain it.
