ABOUT

Kenslee Oxley

Founder & Implementation Specialist

[Kenslee's Photo]

Kenslee started on the ground — running property portfolios, managing tenants, and learning the day-to-day reality of a busy rent roll. Long before systems, technology or consultants were involved.

That hands-on experience is still the lens everything gets filtered through. She knows what it's like when processes break. When team members don't have clarity. When systems slow you down instead of speed you up. When the Principal is the only person who knows how to do half the things that need doing.

From there, she moved into working closely with software providers built for the property industry — learning the platforms from the inside, and spending her days helping property management businesses set them up properly and actually use them.

But she discovered that software alone doesn't fix operational problems.

Neither does advice.

What actually works is getting into the operation, understanding how it really works (not how it's supposed to work), fixing the processes, training the team, holding people accountable, and staying involved until things actually improve.

The OCD PM was built on that approach: real operational experience, genuine software expertise, and a commitment to actual implementation — not just recommendations.
