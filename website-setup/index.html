Run the Online Presence Audit

Check what is happening behind the website

The page customers see is only half the setup. Know who controls the accounts, what is connected and what happens after someone clicks, enquires or orders.

Domain, DNS + hosting

Know your domain name, registrar, renewal date, account owner, hosting provider, nameservers and any subdomains. Check that DNS records are intentional, HTTPS is working and access is protected with two-factor authentication.

Keep the keys safe

Keep the domain, hosting, Google and Shopify logins in a password manager with the right people able to access them. Do not make a DNS change just because a prompt told you to—save a copy of the current settings and check the impact first.

Test the Shopify order

Place a test order from product page to confirmation email. Check stock, shipping, tax, payment, fulfilment, refunds and customer notifications. Decide how often you stop selling a product online when stock or supply changes.

Review Shopify extensions

Review apps for reviews, upsells, subscriptions, email, SMS, search and analytics. Keep the extensions that improve revenue or customer experience, remove the ones adding cost, clutter or slower load times.

Use AI to tidy the setup

I can give you plain-English guidance and prompts to turn your domain, DNS, hosting or Shopify notes into a checklist, spot missing questions and prepare safer changes. AI can help explain and organise the work; verify settings before anyone changes them.
