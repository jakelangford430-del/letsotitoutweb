Brand colour tool

Build a brand palette that makes sense

Start with a practical palette, then adjust it to suit your business. Use the colours across your logo, website, social media, invoices and everyday customer touchpoints.

Colour Palette Tips

Keep it simple: one main colour, one accent, one or two background colours and a readable text colour. The goal is not to collect colours. It is to make your business look like the same business everywhere.

General design guidance only. Check contrast, readability and accessibility before using a palette on customer-facing material, especially websites, forms and invoices.

Create Your Own Palette

Mix and match colours to create a custom palette. Adjust each colour and see how the combination works in a website header, button, social post and invoice.
