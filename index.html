No-BS answers

The sensible questions before you hand your business problems to some bloke on the internet.

What can you actually help me with?

I help untangle messy processes, repetitive admin, disconnected software, unclear roles, inconsistent training and online setup. That might mean improving the tool you already use, creating a clear SOP, connecting systems, building a simple automation, setting up AI for real work or finding a missed follow-up or revenue opportunity.

Do I need to know what system or service I need?

No—and please don’t buy another bit of software just because somebody on TikTok said it would change your life. Tell me what keeps going wrong or wasting time. I’ll help work out whether the answer is software, process, training, automation or something much simpler.

Can you work with the software I already use?

Yes. Replacing everything is usually unnecessary and expensive. I can help clean up your current setup, improve how the tools connect, simplify the way your team uses them and show people what they actually need to know.

Is Let’s Sort It Out only for businesses with employees?

No. You might have a team of 15, one casual who works Tuesdays, or just an ABN and no clear idea where to begin. The advice should suit the size and reality of your business—not pretend you’re running a multinational.

Can you help with websites and online setup?

Yes. I can help with practical website setup, Google Business Profile, Shopify and the basic online systems around your business. The goal is something useful, manageable and easy for customers to understand—not an overpriced digital monument.

Will you tell me if you’re not the right person?

Yes. The first step is understanding what is actually happening. If the problem needs a different specialist, formal advice or a solution outside my lane, I’ll say so plainly.

What happens after I reach out?

You fill in the short form and tell me what’s happening. I’ll review the situation, identify practical gaps or opportunities and come back with a sensible place to start. There is no drawn-out sales process, huge consulting commitment or pressure to buy something you do not need.

Want me to review the mess?

Share what is happening and I’ll point you to the quickest useful fix, whether that is a free tool, a cleanup, automation or hands-on setup.
