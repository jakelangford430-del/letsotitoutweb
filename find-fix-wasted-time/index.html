Find & Fix Your Wasted Time

Most businesses have work getting done twice, data being entered multiple times, or processes that rely on memory instead of logic. It costs real hours and real money. This tool helps you find it, quantify it, and figure out what's worth fixing first.

What You'll Discover

Work through a simple series of questions about your team's daily tasks, common frustrations, and recurring admin work. You'll get back:

  • What you're doing twice (and where the time is really going)
  • Rough cost estimate in hours per week
  • What's worth fixing first based on impact vs effort
  • A prioritized action plan for next steps

How It Works

Answer Questions
