Google Workspace or Microsoft 365?

Both are fine. The right answer is usually whichever one your business already leans on. Paying for both is the common mistake.

Go Google if…

You already live in Gmail, Docs, Sheets and Drive, your team is on Android or mixed devices, and you want the simpler admin screen. Cheaper at the small end.

Go Microsoft if…

You depend on real Excel or Word, you deal with accountants and larger clients who send Office files constantly, or your industry software expects Outlook.

Do not run both

Paying two subscriptions and splitting your files across both is the most common waste I find. Pick one, move properly, cancel the other.

Worth knowing before you touch it

Email is the one thing where a rushed change causes real damage. A wrong record can bounce mail for days, and bounced mail is not recoverable.

  • Never delete the old mailbox until the new one is proven
  • Changing MX records affects mail immediately, not eventually
  • Keep a written copy of the settings before changing them
  • The domain and the email are separate things — you need control of both
  • If your mail already goes to spam, that is a records problem, not a wording problem
