Common questions

Before you book

A few straight answers before we start sorting through the business toolbox.

Is this only for brand-new businesses?

No. It is designed for Australian ABN holders at different stages, including businesses that are already operating but still have gaps, inconsistencies or disconnected systems.

What does the $99 include?

It includes a personal follow-up call, a review of your current setup, a starter mini brand kit, a prioritised action plan, key learnings and relevant guides, checklists or templates selected for your business. The exact resource mix is shaped around what will be most useful.

Does the $99 include a full website or complete business setup?

No. The package provides practical starter outputs and guidance. Larger work such as a complete website, a new logo design, full account configuration, custom document suites or automations can be scoped and quoted separately after we understand what is needed.

What happens after I complete the form?

I will review what you have submitted and reach out to arrange a phone call. We will talk through the business, confirm the most useful focus for your $99 Business in a Box and agree on the next steps.

Is the support limited to one call?

No. The initial call helps me understand what will be useful. Your package also includes the agreed resources and follow-up support to help you understand them. Larger or ongoing implementation is only added if you want it.

Can I implement the recommendations myself?

Absolutely. The resources and action plan are designed to help you move forward yourself. You can also ask for collaborative support or a separate implementation quote where you would rather hand the job over.

Is this legal, tax, financial or HR advice?

No. Business in a Box is practical business setup support and general guidance. It does not replace advice from an accountant, lawyer, Fair Work adviser, safety specialist, privacy professional or licensed provider where that is needed.
